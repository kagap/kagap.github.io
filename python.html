About

Who's behind this?

I'm a data enthusiast who likes turning spreadsheets and public datasets into something people actually want to look at. Right now my toolkit is Power BI and Tableau for dashboards, Python (pandas, matplotlib, Jupyter) for one-off data stories, and SQL for digging into relational data — this site is basically a running log of that. Based in Bytom, Poland, always happy to talk data over email.

Who Are the Bank's Customers?

K-means segmentation of 45,000 bank clients with elbow, silhouette and stability checks, plus an honest business test: the six segments respond differently to a term-deposit offer, but a single yes/no question about a housing loan targets almost as well.

What Should This Listener Hear Next?

A music recommender for 1,900 Last.fm listeners built three ways: popularity, item-item filtering and implicit ALS written from scratch in NumPy. Scored on listening it never saw, personalisation roughly triples the hit rate, and the model learns genres without being told.

Can a Simple Rating Beat the Bookmakers?

Elo ratings built from 27,000 matches in Europe's top five leagues and tested on seasons the model never saw, against Bet365's odds. Ratings alone get most of the way to the bookmakers, and a quietly changing home advantage explains most of the rest.

A machine-learning model on 96,000 Brazilian Olist orders that flags deliveries likely to miss their estimate. Validated on the future, not a random split: the riskiest 20% of orders catch about 40% of all late deliveries.
